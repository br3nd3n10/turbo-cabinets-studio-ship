Turbo Cabinets · A first look

Design your kitchen, then send it to the shop.

This is a customer path, not Noah’s live website. You are trying how a homeowner should start. The real 3D kitchen is not in these pages yet. The point is the conversation before the model.

Turbo needs two things from you so they can model a kitchen that fits your room and looks the way you want.

  1. Your two cabinet walls. One wall has the stove. The other has the sink. Those lengths are what the cabinets have to fit.
  2. A look. Door style and color. You can change this later. It tells Turbo how to dress the boxes.
  3. Send it. In the real app that becomes a job file. Turbo uses it to lay out and model your kitchen. These sketches only pretend to send.

Each card below is a different way to ask those same three things. Open one, click all the way through, then come back and try another. Nothing here changes the live site.
